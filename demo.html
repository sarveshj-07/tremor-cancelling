What am I seeing?
  • The grey ring is where the shaky hand puts the cursor.
  • The coloured dot is what the computer actually shows after filtering.
  • Low-pass and Kalman smooth everything, so the cursor trails behind your hand.
  • AI network learned from simulated tremor to estimate the shake itself and subtract it, so it adds no delay.
